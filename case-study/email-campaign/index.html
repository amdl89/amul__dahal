Email Automation Welcome Flow

Vintage Apparel Brand — Klaviyo Welcome Series

A Klaviyo welcome series for a vintage apparel brand, built around timeless storytelling and soft-conversion strategy.

Project Overview

This project focused on designing and writing a 4-email welcome series for a vintage dresses and accessories brand. The goal was to create a strong first impression for new subscribers while staying true to the brand’s timeless, romantic aesthetic.

The emails were built to welcome new users, communicate brand values, showcase best-selling products, and gently encourage a first purchase through a soft discount strategy.

The Challenge

The brand needed a welcome flow that could:

  • Introduce the brand without feeling overly promotional
  • Reflect the emotional and nostalgic appeal of vintage fashion
  • Build trust with new subscribers unfamiliar with the brand
  • Convert first-time subscribers without aggressive sales tactics

Key Strategy

I approached this project with a story-first, conversion-second mindset.
The strategy was to:

  • Lead with emotion and atmosphere rather than discounts

  • Position the brand as a curator of meaningful, timeless pieces

  • Use soft language and elegant pacing to match the vintage tone

  • Introduce urgency gently, framing discounts as a “welcome gift”

Each email in the sequence serves a clear role while maintaining a consistent voice and visual rhythm.

Overall Flow Rationale:

The welcome sequence was intentionally structured to move from emotion → trust → guidance → action, mirroring how vintage customers typically shop.

Email 1

Welcome + Gift

Objective: First impression, brand positioning, and incentive framing

This email was designed to act as the brand’s first handshake with a new subscriber. Rather than leading aggressively with a discount, the focus was on establishing a sense of warmth, intention, and timelessness — values central to a vintage apparel brand.

The welcome gift is deliberately framed as a thoughtful gesture, not a promotion. Language such as “welcome gift” and “something special” helps maintain the emotional tone while still encouraging an early purchase.

This email introduces the brand’s core promise: curated, enduring pieces chosen for character and craftsmanship. The call-to-action is intentionally simple and inviting, guiding the subscriber toward exploration rather than pushing for an immediate decision.

Why this matters:

A strong, emotionally aligned first email sets expectations for the entire customer relationship and increases trust before any transactional messaging.

Highlights
  • Brand story introduction
  • Welcome discount teaser
  • Soft CTA to explore collections
Email 2

Brand Story

Objective: Emotional connection, differentiation, and trust-building

The second email shifts focus away from selling and toward storytelling and brand philosophy. Its purpose is to help the subscriber understand why the brand exists and what makes it different in a market saturated with fast fashion.

This email leans heavily into nostalgia, intention, and craftsmanship — values that resonate deeply with vintage-focused audiences. Short, reflective sentences are used to slow the reading pace and mirror the brand’s belief in slowing down fashion consumption.

Rather than listing features, the copy highlights beliefs and values, positioning the brand as a curator of meaningful pieces rather than a trend-driven retailer.

Why this matters:

Customers who emotionally connect with a brand are more likely to convert, remain loyal, and perceive products as higher value.

Highlights
  • Craftsmanship & quality focus
  • Sustainability messaging
  • Behind-the-scenes storytelling

Email 3

Best Sellers

Objective: Social proof, guided discovery, and confidence-building

This email is designed to reduce decision fatigue by guiding new subscribers toward proven favorites. Instead of overwhelming the reader with the full catalog, it highlights a small selection of customer-loved pieces, reinforcing trust through social validation.

Product descriptions focus on feeling and longevity rather than technical specifications, aligning with the emotional motivations of vintage shoppers. Limited quantity language subtly reinforces exclusivity without introducing pressure.

The reminder of the welcome gift is placed naturally within the flow, ensuring the incentive supports the decision rather than dominating it.

Why this matters:

Showing what others love helps hesitant buyers feel confident and reassured, especially when shopping from a brand they’re new to.

Highlights
  • Curated product showcase
  • Customer favorites & reviews
  • Direct links to shop
Email 4

Gentle Reminder

Objective: Conversion through soft urgency

The final email introduces urgency while preserving the brand’s calm, elegant tone. Instead of aggressive countdown language, urgency is framed gently, respecting the thoughtful nature of the audience.

This email acknowledges hesitation and reframes the decision as a personal moment — choosing a piece that “felt right” rather than making a rushed purchase. The copy reinforces the idea that these items are meaningful additions to a wardrobe, not impulse buys.

The call-to-action is clear but understated, ensuring the email feels like a reminder rather than a push.

Why this matters:

Urgency drives action, but tone consistency ensures the brand doesn’t break trust at the most critical conversion point.

Highlights
  • Welcome discount reminder
  • Soft urgency messaging
  • Clear call-to-action to purchase

Creating the Flow Trigger

The welcome series begins the moment a visitor subscribes through the on-site signup form. In Klaviyo, this trigger is configured to activate immediately upon form submission, ensuring every new subscriber receives a timely and consistent brand introduction.

The form itself was designed to capture essential information while keeping friction low — just an email address in exchange for a 20% welcome discount. This simplicity encourages higher conversion rates while still enabling personalization in the welcome emails.

By connecting the form directly to the welcome flow, we ensure no subscriber falls through the cracks. The automation handles timing, sequencing, and delivery, allowing the brand to focus on what matters most: building genuine relationships with new customers.

Creating the Flow Automation

The welcome flow was built in Klaviyo with careful attention to timing and sequencing. Each email is spaced to maintain engagement without overwhelming new subscribers.

The automation handles the entire journey — from the moment someone subscribes to delivering the final conversion-focused email. Time delays between emails were calibrated to create a natural rhythm that guides subscribers from introduction to purchase.

This hands-off approach allows the brand to nurture thousands of new relationships simultaneously, while maintaining a personal and intentional tone throughout.

Filtering & Segmentation:

Importantly, emails following the first welcome message are filtered to only reach subscribers who haven't yet placed an order. This prevents unnecessary follow-ups and ensures the sequence remains relevant to each recipient's journey.

At the end of the flow, a custom property named "_Completed_Welcome_Series" is set to true. This allows us to segment subscribers for further relevant actions based on whether the welcome flow converted them or not.
